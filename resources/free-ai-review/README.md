# Free AI Review Sample

A small practical sample from Renteria AI Toolkits for students and small teams reviewing an AI prototype.

## Five questions before sharing an AI demo

1. What specific task and audience does this version support?
2. Can a reviewer trace each material answer to appropriate source evidence?
3. What happens when evidence is missing, contradictory, or untrusted?
4. Which measured failures remain unresolved, and where are the raw results?
5. Who can approve a release, and what is the fallback if review is unavailable?

## Fictional example

A travel assistant quotes a $150 meal cap from a draft even though the approved document says $120. The citation exists, but the source lacks authority. Record a source-authority failure. A possible repair is to use approval status and preserve human review when approved sources conflict. Verify the repair before claiming success.

This authored example is not an executed evaluation. The sample does not certify compliance or production readiness.
